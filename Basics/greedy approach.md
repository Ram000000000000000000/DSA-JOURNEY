#  Greedy Algorithm

---

##  What is Greedy?
Greedy approach makes the **best choice at each step** to achieve overall optimal solution.

---

##  When to Use
- When problem asks for:
  - Maximum / Minimum
  - Best profit
  - Optimal result
- When local choice leads to global optimum

---

##  Key Idea
At every step:
👉 Choose the best possible option **right now**

---

##  Example
Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock:
- Track minimum price
- Calculate maximum profit at each step

---

## ⏱️ Complexity
Usually:
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

---

## ⚠️ Note
Greedy does not work for all problems.  
Sometimes we need Dynamic Programming.

---

## 🚀 Key Learning
Greedy simplifies problems by making optimal choices step-by-step.
